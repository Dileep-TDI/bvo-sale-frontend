# Formal business valuation or valuation software: which do you need?

If you want to know what your business is worth, you have more options than ever. Free online calculators, broker appraisals, valuation software and formal valuations all promise an answer. They are built for different jobs, and choosing the wrong one can cost you.

### Rules of thumb and free calculators

Quick and free, but blunt. They usually apply an industry multiple to revenue or profit, ignoring the risks and strengths that make your business different. They're fine for curiosity, not for decisions.

### Broker appraisals

A broker's appraisal gives a view of what the market might pay. It can be useful, but it isn't independent: a broker wants your listing, and an optimistic number can win it. It also won't usually cover the risks holding your price back.

### Valuation software

Valuation software such as BVOai connects to your accounting software and calculates your value from your live numbers, using the same methodology behind formal valuations. Because it updates as your books reconcile, you can track your value over time, see the risks a buyer would discount you for and benchmark against similar businesses.

It is built for owners who want to grow value over time. What it doesn't include is the professional judgement and sign-off of a formal valuation, so it can't be relied on for tax, legal or court purposes. BVOai starts from $59 a month, with your first week for $1.

### Formal valuations

A formal valuation is prepared by a qualified valuer under the APES 225 professional standard. It is independent, documented and defensible, and includes a detailed review of the risks that affect value. You need one for tax purposes, family law, disputes, restructures, shareholder agreements and many sales.

### Which should you choose?

- **You're one to five years from selling and want to build value:** start with valuation software and track your progress.
- **You need a number you can rely on for tax, a restructure, a dispute or family law:** you need a formal valuation.
- **You're about to go to market:** get a formal valuation so your asking price is defensible and you know where the risks are.
- **You're not sure:** many owners start with a formal valuation for a clear baseline, then track progress in software until they're ready to sell.

### Frequently asked questions

**How much does a business valuation cost?**
It depends on the size and complexity of the business and the purpose of the valuation. Valuation software is a monthly subscription, and a formal valuation is quoted for the job.

**Can I sell using an online valuation?**
It's a good starting point, but buyers and their advisers will test your price. A formal valuation gives you a defensible number to negotiate from.

**How often should I value my business?**
Once a year is a good habit, or continuously with valuation software, plus a formal valuation before any major event such as a sale.
